1. Accent Color/Theme

  Let creators pick a brand color that tints buttons, progress bars, and highlights. Currently everything is black/neutral - a custom color would instantly feel
   personal.

  2. Category Badge

  The category field is fetched but not displayed anywhere. Show it prominently as a tag near the username (e.g., "Fashion & Style" badge).

  3. Featured Content/Portfolio

  Add a gallery section showing actual posts or content thumbnails. Let creators pin their best-performing content.

  4. Rates/Services Section

  Many influencers share pricing. Add fields like:
  - Starting rates for sponsored posts, stories, reels
  - Package pricing
  - "Open for collaborations" status

  5. Custom Links (Linktree-style)

  Add a section for multiple external links (other socials, merch, newsletter, etc.)

  6. Video Introduction

  Short video clip where the creator introduces themselves - very personal touch.

  7. Testimonials/Reviews

  Brands could leave reviews. Social proof from past collaborations.

  8. Contact CTA

  More prominent "Work with me" button - currently website link is subtle.

  9. Custom Section Ordering

  Let users drag-and-drop to reorder sections (show achievements first vs collaborations vs stats).

  10. Background Patterns/Gradients

  Beyond cover image - subtle background patterns or gradient options.

  11. PDF Export

  "Download Media Kit" button that generates a clean PDF.

  12. QR Code

  Shareable QR code for the profile - useful for in-person networking.
